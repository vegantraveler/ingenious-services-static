Industry-Specific Features That Matter for HVAC

Generic AI isn’t good enough for HVAC. Your business has specific terminology, compliance requirements, and customer expectations that generic virtual assistants don’t understand. Here’s how we’re different:

Emergency Triage and Prioritization

When a homeowner calls in a panic because their AC stopped working during a heat advisory, the AI knows to treat this differently than a routine tune-up inquiry. It asks the right triage questions (How long has it been down? Any unusual sounds or smells? Anyone in the home who’s elderly or medically vulnerable?), assigns the appropriate urgency level, and ensures your on-call tech gets dispatched — not the caller sitting at voicemail.

For gas-related calls (possible gas leaks from furnaces or water heaters), the AI is programmed to immediately recommend the caller evacuate and contact their gas utility, then notify your team — a critical safety protocol that a generic AI won’t know to apply.

Seasonal Intelligence

HVAC demand is intensely seasonal. Our system knows this. In July and August, the AI is configured for high-urgency AC emergency protocols. In October and November, it’s optimized for tune-up and system replacement conversations. In January, it shifts to heating emergency mode. You configure the season-aware scripts; the AI executes them automatically.

Maintenance Agreement Management

Your maintenance agreement customers are your highest-lifetime-value clients. The AI knows which customers have active agreements when it pulls their record from ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro, prioritizes their scheduling, and uses that conversation to renew expiring agreements — turning a service booking call into a retention opportunity.

Dispatch and On-Call Integration

During after-hours calls, the AI doesn’t just take a message — it actively dispatches your on-call technician. Integration with your on-call rotation means the right tech gets the right call at the right time, with full customer context and a confirmed appointment time sent to both the tech and the customer.
